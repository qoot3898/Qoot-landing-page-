How we work

From the supplier to your door, we handle every step ourselves.

  1. Source

    From farms and importers we know. Every delivery is checked at our door.

  2. Store

    Our Benghazi warehouse keeps dry, chilled and frozen apart, oldest out first.

  3. Cook

    Daily meals for canteens, site camps and events, sealed in Qoot bowls.

  4. Deliver

    Daily in Benghazi, and on a fixed schedule to cities and sites across Libya.

What the name means

In Arabic, qoot (قوت) is the food a household needs each day. Qoot Al Madinah is the city’s daily food.

Benghazi, Libya

Which areas do you deliver to?

We deliver across Benghazi every day, and to cities and work sites all over Libya on a fixed schedule, from Tripoli to Tobruk and down to Sabha and Kufra. Tell us your location and we’ll confirm the run.

Is there a minimum order?

Yes. It depends on the delivery distance, and we state it clearly in your quote.

Is all your meat halal?

Yes. All meat and poultry we supply or cook is halal, and we can share supplier certificates on request.

Can you cater a one-off event?

Yes. We cook for weddings, conferences and company events. Give us at least a week’s notice and the number of guests.

Can you run the kitchen at our site camp?

Yes. We can supply your kitchen with ingredients only, or send our cooks to run it with full-board meals.

How is a contract priced?

Catering is priced per person per day, and supplies by price list. We send a written quote within two working days of your request.
